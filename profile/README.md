## 简体中文

### 关于我们

Sakuria Project 是 [Sakuria](https://sakuria.syarolia.com/) 背后的独立产品与工程团队。我们围绕插画、漫画、动图与小说的发现和阅读体验，持续建设 Web、iOS 客户端及其服务端基础设施。

Sakuria 专注于「舒适浏览」：柔和的配色、流畅的动效、贴心的阅读器与真实逐帧动图播放，让用户更愉快地发现喜欢的创作者与作品。

### 我们在做什么

- **作品发现**：插画、漫画、动图与小说浏览
- **内容探索**：多维排行榜、作品与创作者搜索、高级筛选
- **沉浸阅读**：小说章节、注音、主题、字号与导出体验
- **个人空间**：关注、收藏、点赞、浏览历史与随机漫游
- **跨端建设**：持续打磨 Sakuria Web、iOS 与支撑服务

## English

### Who we are

Sakuria Project is the independent product and engineering team behind [Sakuria](https://sakuria.syarolia.com/). We build the web, iOS, and backend experiences that help people discover and enjoy illustrations, manga, ugoira, and novels.

Sakuria is focused on comfortable browsing: soft colours, smooth motion, a thoughtful reader, and true frame-by-frame ugoira playback make discovering creators and works more enjoyable.

### What we build

- Browsing experiences for illustrations, manga, ugoira, and novels
- Multi-dimensional rankings, search, and advanced filters
- A focused novel reader with chapters, ruby, themes, typography, and export
- Follows, bookmarks, likes, browsing history, and random discovery
- Sakuria Web, an iOS client, and the services behind them
